## Problem

Dating apps in India are photo-first: you match on a jawline, open chat, and hit a wall. The "hey black hole": matched, said hi, silence, unmatch, repeat. For Gen Z building a social graph from scratch, the apps are exhausting or unsafe. Either you perform for strangers or you don't show up at all.

## Solution

Building a matchmaking experience where real connection comes before photos and performance.
