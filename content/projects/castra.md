## Problem

Performance marketers were making million-dollar ad decisions on gut instinct and recycled hooks, while the real signal lived scattered across reviews, Reddit, ad comments, and support tickets. After iOS 14 killed targeting, creative quality became the last competitive edge, and brands had no systematic way to build it.

## Solution

Castra ingests raw customer language from Amazon reviews, Reddit, TikTok ad comments, support tickets, and post-purchase surveys, then turns it into structured creative strategy. It clusters signals, classifies objections, desires, and buying triggers, and generates ranked ad angles, hooks, objection maps, and UGC briefs grounded in verbatim evidence. The intelligence layer between what customers say and what your creative team builds next.
