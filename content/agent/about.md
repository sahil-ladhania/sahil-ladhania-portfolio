# About Sahil Ladhania

Sahil Ladhania is a full-stack and AI engineer based in India (IST). He writes TypeScript, designs systems that hold up in production, and builds AI features grounded in real data—not slide-deck demos.

He studied business (BBA), not computer science. He got into engineering by solving his own problems: tutorials, small scripts, breaking and fixing things until he was shipping full products. Business school taught him how companies work—who pays, what breaks at scale, why clean handoffs matter.

Today he splits time between client work through Zyntohouse and products he is building himself. He is strongest where backend systems, AI pipelines, and product decisions overlap.
